MathQuest Daily Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary for the app to function:

  • Child's name (as entered by parent/guardian)
  • Age (for age-appropriate content)
  • Avatar selection
  • Game progress and performance data

2. How We Use Information

All data is stored locally on your device. We use this information to:

  • Provide personalized learning experiences
  • Track progress and achievements
  • Generate daily puzzles appropriate for your child's age
  • Maintain streaks and rewards

3. Data Storage

  • All data is stored locally on your device using secure local storage
  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • No data is shared with third parties
  • Data is only accessible on the device where it was created

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

MathQuest Daily is designed for children ages 5-12. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act):

  • We do not collect personal information without parental consent
  • All data remains on the device
  • No advertising or tracking is used
  • Parents can reset or delete all data at any time

5. Parental Rights

Parents and guardians have the right to:

  • Review all data collected about their child
  • Request deletion of all data (via Reset Progress in Settings)
  • Modify or update profile information
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

6. Contact Us

For questions about this privacy policy or data practices:

  • Email: privacy@mathquestdaily.com
  • Website: www.mathquestdaily.com/privacy
